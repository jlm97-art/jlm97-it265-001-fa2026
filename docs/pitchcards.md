

# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./01-idea-bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card 1 (Physical)

**Working title: Freaky Taxi**

**Player role and situation: Before starting the game, players must roll a set of dice to determine player order, and then spin a given wheel to determine player quota. Players must take turns to move spaces, whether it be from dice rolls or board space events, to reach the exit first while maintaining a quota (in this game's case, money) to unlock the exit gate.**

**Repeated decision or action: A repeated decision of this game is rolling a given dice, whether the default or optional item dice to progress through the board.**

**Goal, pressure, or ending: The goal of this board game is to meet the given quota and to reach the board's exit gate before the other players.**

**Hook: Items aplenty to create chaos every turn**

**Smallest useful physical prototype: Sketched board on white paper, chess pawns for player, paperclip attached to cut out circular cardboard for quota wheel, set of dice for primary and item dice sets**

**Question I want listeners to answer: In such a game do you prioritize getting to the end first, or meeting the quota first knowing your competitors are right behind you?**

## Pitch Card 2 (Digital)

**Working title: Eviction Notice**

**Player role and situation: Players must navigate their character to reach the minimum quota before the time runs out to advance to the next round while fending off enemies that get stronger each round.**

**Repeated decision or action: A repeated decision of this game is choosing which enemies to target first as they all vary in strength and speed.**

**Goal, pressure, or ending: The goal is to meet the minimum quota before the time runs out, and the pressure of this game is the enemies growing stronger per passed round.**

**Hook: Luck, free-roam combat, upgrade paths**

**Smallest useful physical prototype: Red magnet as the player, other magnets surrounding the red magnet as the enemies**

**Question I want listeners to answer: Do you take a defensive, or offensive approach?**
