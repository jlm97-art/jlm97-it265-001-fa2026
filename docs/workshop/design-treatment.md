## Game Concept

<!-- Explain why the working title fits. Mention alternative titles if useful. -->
[I decided on the name "Freaky Taxi" for this strategic board game as it aims to capture the chaotic day-to-day experience
of trying to get around large cities (New York as a prominent example). The title is a first impression that the board game
will try to replicate the feeling of trying to haul a cab, scrounging enough money for a bus fare, or simply just trying to
get to the next train on time through board and dice.]
<!-- Describe the setting, theme, and story or premise. Identify the characters
or the role the player takes. A game without a story can describe its situation
and player roles instead; do not invent a narrative just to fill this section. -->
[In Freaky Taxi, players compete against one another (or in teams) to collect the required amount of currency coins in order to
access the exit gate - or in the case of this game's setting, the taxi - before the other players (or other team) manage to do so. In story terms, players must acquire
enough money to pay for the last taxi home. The themes of this board game are wealth acquisition, exploration, and strategy,]

## Gameplay

<!-- Explain the player's objectives, the conflicts or challenges, and how play
succeeds, fails, or ends. Describe the recurring decisions and actions, the rules
that connect them, and what changes in the game as a result. Game state means the
current situation, such as positions, resources, score, or available choices.
Describe the intended experience through those interactions. -->
[Goals: Acquiring enough tokens to unlock the exit gate first, accumulating a set of item dice to help the player with collecting currencies, moving spaces,
and sabotaging other players
Ending: When a player first reaches the exit taxi before all other players. 
In team-based gameplay, the player from one team that reaches the end first wins the game for that team.
Challenges: Maintaining a sizeable amount of currency coins and item dice sets, adapting to player sabotage strategies and random chance events from tiles, keeping close to the exit gate,
strategizing for when other players may be at a noticeable advantage]
 
[Explain the core loop: what the player considers, chooses or does, and how that changes the next decision.
The player considers their current inventory of items, what space they are on the map layout, and where other players are in order to strategize based on what
they currently have, what other players have, and where they currently are. The player also also may choose to land on a certain spot, or use a specific dice item
to mitigate any negative effects imposed by other players on the next coming turns.]
### Setup And Start

<!-- Describe the starting state: how the play area and pieces are arranged,
what each player begins with, and how play starts. Refer to the pieces described
below rather than listing them twice. -->
[A game is first set up by deciding turn order. Each player rolls a D-20, and turn order is decided based on ascending dice value. The person who rolls the
highest dice value goes first. A spinner is then used to place the exit spots on the map, from 1 to 30 for each of the 3 islands.]
